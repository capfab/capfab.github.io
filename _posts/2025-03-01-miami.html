--- title: "Miami, Florida" date: 2025-03-01 categories: [travel] image: /assets/img/notes_images/miami-1.jpg ---

This was the last trip before flying back to Vietnam for the VinRobotics job, more a bonding trip with a few close friends than a sightseeing one. Most of it ended up being about the food. Latin food in Miami is genuinely something else, a little oily maybe, but everything hit exactly where it needed to. The beaches were warm and the sand was coarser than I expected, though still better than anything on the Jersey shore.

We spent an afternoon in Little Havana, walked through the domino park to take photos of the games going on at the tables, ducked into a couple of cigar shops. We ended up walking most places since Uber was pricier than expected, which wasn't really a complaint, Miami is a decent city to just walk through.

The moment that actually stuck with me was getting back in the ocean. I hadn't swum in almost two years, not since I left Vietnam in 2023, and the cruise port happened to be right next to where I was in the water, so I spent a while just watching the ships take turns coming in and out while I floated around.

Vizcaya was worth the detour. The whole place makes a lot more sense once you know it was a winter retreat: the outlook post over the bay, the stage, the mangroves, the little maze all feel like they were built for exactly that kind of unhurried life. We closed out the trip with a boat tour past the row of billionaire villas on the water, and the sunset on the way back was genuinely magnificent. That stretch of the city would be a nicer place to actually live in if it weren't priced for people who don't have to think about it.

Nothing about the trip felt like something to skip. I'd go back for a different set of things to do rather than to redo any of it.

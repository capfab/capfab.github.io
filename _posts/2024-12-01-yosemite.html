My first trip to California, out to see two friends from undergrad, Hung Nguyen and Long-Giang Vu. I flew into San Diego first and stayed a few days, then got to Yosemite the unglamorous way: a three-leg train-and-bus combination up through Los Angeles and Bakersfield to Merced, then the YARTS bus the rest of the way in. We packed sandwiches for the ride so none of us had to deal with finding food along the way.

The original plan was to stay through Christmas so we'd catch snow, but we ended up cutting it short and headed back before the holiday. The three of us split a wooden cabin, ate pretty basic stuff the whole time (pizza, eggs, bacon, burgers), and never did get the snow we were hoping for. It rained instead, wet and gloomy most of the days we were there.

Yosemite didn't care that the weather wasn't cooperating. It was still grand, still out of this world, even soaked. We ended up on the Four Mile Trail since the Mist Trail and the Nevada Trail were both closed from the rain and the snowfall the nights before. The waterfalls that afternoon were throwing off actual rainbows through a fire-orange mist as the sun went down, which is one of those things that's hard to believe until you're standing in front of it.

Everywhere we went felt pristine and completely quiet in a way that made me appreciate the National Park Service more than I expected to going in. I still owe myself the trails we didn't get to.

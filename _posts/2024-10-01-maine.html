Went out to Bar Harbor and Acadia with Xuanhao Ong and his family, timed for what we thought would be peak fall color. We were a week or two late and missed the real peak, but it barely mattered. The cold was exactly the kind I like, and the whole place still felt breathtaking without needing to be complicated about it.

Watched the sunset from the top of Cadillac Mountain, made the stop at the lighthouse from Forrest Gump, and ate well pretty much everywhere we went. The lobster roll at Young's lived up to its reputation completely, no notes there. If I'm honest I wish we'd timed it a week or two earlier for the color, but that's really the only complaint I have. I'll go back.

About

I lead the Reinforcement Learning Department at VinRobotics in Hanoi, where I also work hands-on, taking locomotion and loco-manipulation policies from simulation onto real humanoids. We build robots that walk, carry, recover their balance, and adapt to terrain and context, all under payload. Before this, I did my MSE in Robotics at Penn, and my undergraduate thesis at HCMUT was a structured-light vision system for robotic bin-picking.

I care deeply about the role perception and world understanding play in robot behavior, because what a robot can do depends on what it can see and make sense of. My research focuses on closing the gap between what a robot perceives and what it can safely do. Ultimately, I want to explore the fundamental skills future robots will need, whole-body control and loco-manipulation, grounded in the context of their environment.

I'm applying to PhD programs in robot learning focused on these topics. In the meantime, I'm also open to full-time opportunities and research collaborations, so please reach out if that's you.

Outside the lab, I'm usually the one already looking at flights for the next trip. More in News.

Research

TD-MTP: Coupling Structured Planning with Multi-Step Value Learning Under review

Model-based agents tune the planner that chooses actions and the target the critic learns from separately. A paired study across ten tasks shows the two interact: the multi-step TD(λ) target alone beats the baseline on eight tasks, the structured tensor-graph planner alone on one, and on the stair task each change alone hurts while the two together finish well above baseline. 10 wins, 22 ties, and 6 losses across 38 tasks.

Terrain Traversal Without Motion Priors: Expert-Routed Humanoid Locomotion at Deployment Fidelity Under review

Perceptive humanoid locomotion without the usual motion-capture and distillation pipeline. A terrain classifier routes a five-expert mixture over a sensed elevation grid, and every foothold target is refined by closed-form gradient steps on an interpolated terrain-cost field rather than snapped to a fixed grid. Crosses an 85 m five-segment waypoint course 18/20 end-to-end in a second, deployment-fidelity simulator.

Project page →

TACT-ful: Multi-Channel Terrain Affordance and Compliance Training for Payload-Robust Perceptive Humanoid Locomotion Under review

A perceptive locomotion system for payload-robust humanoid walking. Multi-channel terrain cost maps guide a GPU-parallel foothold planner, with virtual wrench injection enabling payload-compliance training without force sensors. Achieves 1.0 m/s on stairs with 20 cm risers and zero-shot transfer to a physical humanoid carrying up to 20 kg.

Project page →

Whole-Body Compliance for Heavy Humanoids via Force Latent Estimation and Residual Impedance Targets Under review

Whole-body compliance for a 70 kg heavy humanoid under high-payload and external-force scenarios. A multi-site impedance reference controller, variational force latent encoder, and bounded residual mechanism jointly edit impedance equilibria, validated on surface wiping, force reaction, loaded squatting, and cooperative transport with a 0.98 success rate.
